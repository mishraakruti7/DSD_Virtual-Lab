## 2. What Personal Data We Collect and Why (Purpose Specification)
We only collect personal information that is necessary for you to use the Virtual Lab and complete your academic requirements:

1. **Your College Email and Name:**
   - **Why:** To create your login account, verify your enrollment in the course, send password reset links, and display your name on lab certificates and faculty grading sheets.
   - **Legal Basis:** Your explicit consent given when creating your account.

2. **Your Coursework Progress (Labs 1–12 and Theory Modules):**
   - **Why:** To track which experiments and reading modules you have completed, and to calculate your continuous assessment grades for the course.
   - **Legal Basis:** Performance of academic course obligations and your consent.

3. **Your Quiz Attempts and Scores:**
   - **Why:** To evaluate your understanding of digital logic topics and provide automated feedback mapped to Course Outcomes (CO1–CO6).
   - **Legal Basis:** Course continuous evaluation and your consent.

4. **Your Saved Circuits:**
   - **Why:** To save your breadboard wirings and IC simulations so you can continue your practical experiments across different devices.
   - **Legal Basis:** Your consent and functional necessity.

5. **AI Tutor Messages (Optional):**
   - **Why:** When you ask questions to the "Ask the DSD Tutor" feature, your questions are processed to provide helpful step-by-step guidance.
   - **Legal Basis:** Your explicit opt-in before using the tutor. You can turn off chat history saving or delete all past conversations at any time.

6. **Feedback and Support Messages:**
   - **Why:** To respond to your questions and resolve reported technical glitches.
   - **Legal Basis:** Legitimate course support with your consent.

> **What We Do NOT Collect:** We do not collect your phone number, date of birth, postal address, religion, caste, biometric information, precise GPS location, or device fingerprints. We do not store student profile photos (avatars are shown using your initials).

## 3. Who Else Receives Your Information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only use reputable service providers ("Data Processors") strictly to operate the platform:
- **Supabase Inc.**: Hosts our database and handles user authentication securely in compliant data centres.
- **Anthropic PBC**: Processes text queries sent to the DSD Tutor AI assistant via secure backend Edge Functions. Personal names, emails, and passwords are never sent to Anthropic.
- **Google LLC**: Provides optional Single Sign-On (OAuth). Only basic profile information (email and name) is exchanged.
- **Hosting Provider (Vercel / Cloudflare)**: Delivers web pages securely over encrypted HTTPS.

All data transmissions use modern TLS 1.3 encryption.

## 4. Visibility to Course Faculty and Administrators
Course instructors and departmental administrators can view your name, email, experiment completion records, quiz scores, and saved breadboard circuits. This visibility is necessary for grading your semester laboratory work and conducting continuous assessments. Faculty members cannot view your private passwords. Every time faculty or administrators access student records or export class rosters, an entry is permanently written to an administrative audit log.

---

## 5. Storage Region and How Long We Keep Your Data (Retention)
- **Data Region:** Data is stored in secure database facilities (with regional servers in Mumbai, India where enabled).
- **Active Accounts:** Data is kept for the duration of your course enrollment plus one additional academic year for accreditation audits.
- **AI Tutor History:** Chat messages are automatically purged after 90 days.
- **Support Inquiries:** Resolved help tickets are anonymised after 12 months.
- **Unverified Accounts:** Unverified signups are deleted automatically after 7 days.
- **Account Deletion:** If you delete your account through the `/my-data` page, all your personal records are permanently erased within 30 days.

## 6. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act (How to Exercise Them)
Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have clear rights regarding your personal information:
1. **Right to Access:** You can review all data we hold about you or download a complete copy in JSON format at [/my-data](file:///my-data).
2. **Right to Correction & Updating:** You can update your profile display name at any time.
3. **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You can delete your account and all associated lab work through [/my-data](file:///my-data).
4. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdrawing your consent is as simple as granting it. You can withdraw consent from the `/my-data` page (this will deactivate cloud sync for future sessions).
5. **Right of Grievance Redressal:** If you believe your data has been mishandled, you can submit an inquiry to our Grievance Officer at [GRIEVANCE OFFICER EMAIL]. We will acknowledge and respond within statutory timelines.
6. **Right to Nominate:** In the event of death or incapacity, you have the right to nominate another individual to manage your digital data rights by contacting the Grievance Officer.

## 7. Age Requirement (Students Under 18 Years)
This portal is designed for higher-education engineering students who are 18 years of age or older. By signing up, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. If you are under 18 years of age, you must have verifiable parental or guardian consent before creating an account, in accordance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act.

## 8. Security Measures and Breach Notification
We protect your data with Row Level Security (RLS) policies, session encryption, strict role-based access controls, and rate limiters. In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that poses risk of harm, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and affected users without delay, in compliance with statutory breach reporting guidelines.
